How to play

  • Each player starts in a corner: Blue top-left, Yellow top-right, Red bottom-right, Green bottom-left. Your first piece must cover your corner square.
  • Every piece after that must touch your own colour diagonally (corner-to-corner) at least once — and must never touch your own colour along an edge.
  • Touching other colours is fine, edge or corner. Pieces may not overlap or hang off the board.
  • If you have no legal move, you pass. The game ends when nobody can move.
  • Scoring: −1 point for every square of every piece you couldn't place. Placing all 21 pieces earns +15, and +5 more if your last piece was the single square.

How to win — 5 classic tips
  • Head for the centre early. The middle of the board is where territory is won; play your big pieces toward it in the opening.
  • Expand in more than one direction. Every new corner your pieces create is a future foothold — a shape with many open corners stays flexible.
  • Block opponents' corners. Covering a square an opponent needs for corner-contact can strand a whole branch of their territory.
  • Save small pieces for last. The single square, domino and small corners are your endgame tools for tight gaps. Spend the big pieces while space is open.
  • Count your remaining squares. Your score is minus the squares you leave behind — a 5-square piece left unplayed costs five times a domino.
